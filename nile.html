The Nile · Cairo
01Life along the water

A ribbon of green
through the desert.

From the air, the Nile looks like a green thread drawn through sand. Along its banks, fertile fields and busy towns give way to palms, temples, and open desert.

Take a slow felucca ride and the scale of the landscape changes: the river becomes a place to pause, watch the light, and see daily life drift by.

On the water · Upper Egypt

A slower way to see

Let the river set the pace.

Feluccas are traditional wooden sailboats that have traveled these waters for generations. With no engine noise, a river sail is a simple way to take in the breeze, the banks, and the changing colors of the sky.
