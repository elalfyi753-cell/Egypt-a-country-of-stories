Giza Plateau · Cairo
01A timeless landmark

Built for eternity.
Still full of life.

The Giza Plateau is home to the Great Pyramid, the Pyramid of Khafre, the Pyramid of Menkaure, and the Sphinx. Together, they form one of the world’s most recognizable archaeological landscapes.

These monuments were built as royal tombs during Egypt’s Old Kingdom. Their precise construction and lasting presence continue to inspire questions and admiration.

A closer look

Stone, sky,
and deep time.

Up close, the pyramid is not a smooth shape but a mountain of enormous limestone blocks. Look beyond the famous outline and notice the craft, the patterns of light, and the desert all around it.
